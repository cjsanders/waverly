---
title: Workspaces
description: Creator, brand, and operator modes, their URLs, and how to switch.
---

A workspace is a WorkOS organization. Its `metadata.kind` decides which mode of the affiliate app it opens in. You can belong to several workspaces and switch between them; the active WorkOS organization on your session is the one the app uses.

## Modes

| Kind     | Home        | What it is for                                                                                     |
| -------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Creator  | `/creator`  | Share links, track sales, and get paid for what you promote.                                       |
| Brand    | `/brand`    | Run campaigns, recruit creators, and pay commissions.                                              |
| Operator | `/operator` | Network-wide brands, creators, and payouts. Provisioned by Waverly, not [onboarding](/onboarding). |

Organizations without a valid `kind` are ignored at sign-in and cannot open the app.

## Routes

Each mode lives under its own prefix. Visiting a prefix that does not match the active workspace sends you to that workspace's home. `/dashboard` signs you in if needed, then opens the [compact affiliate workspace](/affiliate-workspace) — discovery, reports, and messages for the selected demo identity.

If you have memberships but the session has no active organization yet — a fresh sign-up, or a membership added elsewhere — the app selects your first workspace and shows “Opening …” while the session updates.

## Switcher

The sidebar control shows the active workspace name and its kind (for example, “Creator workspace”). Open it to:

- See every workspace you belong to, with kind and role
- Switch to another workspace (the URL prefix and navigation change)
- Create another workspace via [onboarding](/onboarding)

Workspaces in the list are ordered operator, then brand, then creator, and alphabetically within each kind.

## Navigation

WorkOS mode sidebars still treat most items as “Coming soon.” The signed-in [affiliate workspace](/affiliate-workspace) is the compact dashboard with discovery, reports, and messages. Each WorkOS mode also has its own primary action in the top bar.

| Mode     | Sidebar                                       | Primary action | Empty overview                                                                     |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Creator  | Overview, Campaigns, Links, Payouts, Reports  | New link       | Sales, commissions, and campaign performance appear once data starts flowing.      |
| Brand    | Overview, Campaigns, Creators, Links, Reports | New campaign   | Creator activity, conversions, and campaign spend appear once data starts flowing. |
| Operator | Overview, Brands, Creators, Payouts, Reports  | Invite brand   | Network-wide brands, creators, and payouts appear once data starts flowing.        |

The overview card title is **Nothing to show yet** until those pages have data.
