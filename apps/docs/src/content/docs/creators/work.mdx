Work pages cover production and the links you publish.

## Collaborations

| Page          | What it is for                                                                                           |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Opportunities | Complete briefs from publishers that match your formats, audience, and rates.                            |
| Projects      | Move a locked brief through creation, publisher review, and approval.                                    |
| Portfolio     | Proof of work so publishers can see voice, formats, and audience.                                        |
| Partnerships  | Brand relationships, products, samples, announcements, campaigns, and loyalty programs you already have. |
| Placements    | Flat-fee content: contracts, deliverables, and a creator-facing rate card.                               |

## Links and storefront

| Page       | What it is for                                                                      |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tracking   | Create and organize product links, storefront routes, archives, and discount codes. |
| Storefront | Curate products, posts, and folders on your public shoppable profile.               |

Tracking links stay stable for readers. If a destination or provider changes, Waverly records a new immutable link version instead of silently rewriting the old one.

Publishers and properties on the organization sidebar are the media companies and sites that commission or distribute your work. [Messages](/creators/workspace) keep those conversations next to the brief.
