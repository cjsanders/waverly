Discovery is how you decide what to promote before you create a link or apply to a collaboration.

| Page             | What it is for                                                                                              |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| For you          | Ranked opportunities from audience fit, live terms, and recent performance.                                 |
| Product catalog  | Compare products, commercial terms, ratings, samples, and audience fit.                                     |
| Brand catalog    | Open a brand to review products, partnership status, terms, and performance.                                |
| Cost-per-click   | Active click campaigns with remaining budget and a strong audience match.                                   |
| Loyalty programs | Brand bonuses and how close you are to unlocking them.                                                      |
| Lists            | Editorial collections for upcoming coverage. Saved product lists persist in the browser for this workspace. |

Save products from catalog views when you want them later. Those selections stay in the browser for the active workspace; they are not a network-wide library.

When you are ready to promote something, move to [Work](/creators/work) to create tracking links, manage partnerships, or apply to a brief.
