Creators work in a **creator workspace**. That workspace is a Waverly organization whose kind is creator. You can belong to more than one workspace and switch among them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the affiliate app and sign in. If you have no workspace yet, Waverly sends you to
    onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a creator workspace">
    Choose **Creator**, name the workspace (your name or channel, 2–80 characters), and create it.
    Waverly makes you the owner and opens `/creator`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Overview">
    Overview is the home for briefs, deadlines, and payments as activity starts to flow. Other
    sidebar pages stay available even when a given surface still shows sample or empty state copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The URL keeps `page` (the sidebar item). A missing or unknown page falls back to Overview. On Messages, `thread` is the open conversation.
