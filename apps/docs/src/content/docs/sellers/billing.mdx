Billing shows the consolidated monthly invoice for this brand workspace: platform fees, creator commissions, and paid placements.

Open invoices stay payable until they are marked paid. Commission lines follow the conversions already attributed in [Reporting](/sellers/reporting); placement lines follow approved paid placements.

Workspace-level payment methods and invoice contacts live under [Settings](/sellers/workspace).
