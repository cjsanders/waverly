Creator pages are how you find partners and run the relationship after they apply.

| Page              | What it is for                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Creator directory | Publishers, influencers, media buyers, and deal sites, with audience and performance evidence.  |
| Applications      | Inbound applications. Approving one grants access to your products and public terms.            |
| Partnerships      | Active partners, groups, and private commission rates.                                          |
| Paid placements   | Flat-fee content from rate card and proposal through agreement, escrow, delivery, and approval. |

Applications are the gate into public terms. Partnerships are the ongoing commercial relationship, including private rates. Paid placements are project work with a fixed fee, not a catalog commission.

Negotiate in [Messages](/sellers/workspace) so the brief and the thread stay in the same workspace.
