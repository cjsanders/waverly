Sellers work in a **brand workspace**. In the product this is labeled a brand organization; these docs call that role Seller.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the affiliate app and sign in. If you have no workspace yet, Waverly sends you to
    onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a brand workspace">
    Choose **Brand**, name the workspace (your company or program, 2–80 characters), and create it.
    Waverly makes you the owner and opens `/brand`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Overview">
    Overview is the home for sales, creator decisions, campaign spend, and program health. Other
    sidebar pages stay available even when a given surface still shows sample or empty state copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The URL keeps `page` (the sidebar item). A missing or unknown page falls back to Overview. On Messages, `thread` is the open conversation.

Next: set up the [program](/sellers/program), then [recruit creators](/sellers/creators).
