`/onboarding` is where you create a workspace. You land here automatically when you are signed in and have none. The sidebar switcher also links **New workspace** so you can add another later.

## Create a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a type">
    **Creator** — share links, track sales, and get paid for what you promote.

    **Brand** — run campaigns, recruit creators, and pay commissions.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Two to 80 characters. The placeholder is “Your name or channel” for a creator and “Acme Outdoors” for a brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create workspace">
    Waverly creates a WorkOS organization with that `kind`, makes you the `owner`, syncs your memberships, switches the session to the new organization, and opens its [home](/workspaces).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The form title is **Set up your workspace** when you have none, and **Create another workspace** when you already belong to at least one. The copy notes that you can belong to more than one.

<Aside type="note" title="Operator workspaces">
  Onboarding cannot create an operator workspace. Those organizations are provisioned by Waverly.
  See [WorkOS setup](/workos).
</Aside>
